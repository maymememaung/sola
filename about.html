Our story

Sola started with a simple observation: most cafés are too loud to actually think in. Conversations spill over, music competes with your headphones, and the table nearest the espresso machine shakes every few minutes. We wanted something different.

We opened Sola as a dedicated space for people who need to get serious work done — students pulling long study sessions, freelancers who've outgrown their home office, and remote professionals who crave a change of scenery without the noise that usually comes with it. Every design decision, from the acoustic paneling on the walls to the warm-toned task lighting above each desk, was made with focus in mind.

The coffee and food came second, but we took them just as seriously. We source single-origin beans from small roasters, brew matcha from ceremonial-grade leaves, and keep the food menu short so that everything on it is genuinely good.

What we stand for

  • Quiet by design

    Sola is a silent café. No phone calls, no video meetings without headphones, no loud conversations. It is a rule we enforce gently because our regulars depend on it.

  • Unhurried time

    There are no time limits on seats. Order when you're ready, stay as long as you need. We believe good work takes as long as it takes.

  • Quality over quantity

    A short menu done well beats a long menu done indifferently. The same goes for our space: we keep fewer seats so each one is actually comfortable.
